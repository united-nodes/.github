# United Nodes of Anonymity

We are a loose collective of developers, cryptographers, researchers and users focused on: 
- Cryptography and network privacy
- Anonymous systems
- Open source software
- Anti-censorship and free speech

We write and maintain the code. We don't have a leader, and anyone can contribute to our projects.

Our goal is to build a truly equal and free network. [Read our Manifesto](../MANIFESTO.txt)

## FAQ

### Q: Who are you?

A: We are a loose collective committed to building a free network ecosystem. Members are called "Nodes". We pursue the anonymity and privacy of the network. We are who we are.

### Q: Why anonymity is important?

A: Because it can ensure that others (no matter who) will not track your true identity and ensure your safety and freedom. Others can only know "there is someone here", without knowing any information about you.

### Q: What is your goal?

A: Our goal is to build a open, free and equal network without leaders or capital. To learn more, read our Manifesto.

### Q: Can I join?

A: Of course. If you have the same beliefs as us, we welcome you to join us at any time. If you want to leave, you don't need any notice too.

### Q: Why don't you recommend cryptocurrency?

A: Although the technology of cryptocurrency itself is good, due to the inequality of resources in reality, it is exacerbating the gap between rich and poor. The equality we pursue is true equality.

### Q: Do you have any sponsors?

A: Absolutely not. We are a completely independent network. And because of our structure, it is difficult for you to sponsor or donate to us.

### Q: How can I contribute to these projects?

A: Just open a pull request. In order to prevent attacks, some repositories use Proof of Work (PoW) to prevent spam. In addition, all your contributions will be placed in the Public Domain, which means that you need to give up the copyright of your contributions and allow others to use them without restrictions. If you don't want this, please fork the project and publish it with another license after modifying its source code.

### Q: Do you have a symbol?

A: We propose the symbol `⦱`. In mathematics, this symbol represents "the complement of the empty set", that is, the universal set. 

### Q:... So, what time is it now?

A: It's time to change the Internet!
