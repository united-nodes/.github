The Manifesto of the United Nodes

The internet is no longer what it once was. It has been divided into platforms,
becoming a hunting ground for capital. Some people are content with the status
quo, or rather they are the managers of those corporations, and they do not
think this is a bad thing. Others try to change it, replacing a single social
platform with federated communities made up of servers, then calling this
"decentralization". Still others try to establish a new model using
cryptocurrency and blockchain, but this still relies on your resources and
reputation in reality. Taken together, all these are the so-called Web3.

We hereby remind these people: you do not pursue true freedom at all, or you
fail to recognize it. A server will always have an administrator, and this is
hard to change. It is the early investors in cryptocurrency, including those
speculators, who get the returns. Ordinary users can do nothing but watch from
the sidelines. This is essentially an iron rule: only if you have money can you
have tokens, and only then do you have a voice.

The internet's original wish was to build a network that was truly decentralized,
open and free. But look at the internet now: platforms guard their trade secrets,
while cryptocurrency widens the gap between rich and poor. A certain number of
users are aggregated onto a single server. All that is left within the network
is this, and the places of true freedom are becoming fewer and fewer, nearly
disappearing.

As for these problems in reality, let the communists and anarchists solve them.
As for us -- independent Nodes unto ourselves -- we will create a new world upon
the ruins of this cyberspace, a network that is truly decentralized and equal.

After the cypherpunk movement of the 1990s, we emerged. And now, we still pursue
privacy, connecting with each other in some autonomous way, becoming a fork of
the internet. We are the United Nodes of Anonymity. However, the ideas of the
old era no longer fully suit the current state of the internet, and we must
advance with the times.

The freedom of information is still indispensable, but freedom among Nodes is
equally important. On the basis of using cryptographic algorithms, we have
completely abandoned reliance on any single protocol or system. We exchange
information by any viable means, whether web pages, email, or Tor packets. The
structure has been thoroughly broken up, and this is exactly what we want,
because it makes connections untraceable. Every Node has sovereignty, and the
connections between them are voluntary and autonomous, so we have no leaders,
let alone a center.

Trust between people is not reliable, so we use cryptography instead. Because an
unforgeable signature is more effective than a promise. We reject the illusion
of blockchain: this is not freedom at all, but rather a form of restriction.
Governance based on it is even more of a mistake, because everything is made
public, and we do not need this thing either. We are merely a loose collection
formed by the connections of Nodes, not even to be called an organization.

Names, including the direct and indirect benefits that names bring, are utterly
useless here. True anonymity is letting no one know who you are, while declaring
your existence. This is not merely a problem that can be solved at the protocol
level. Therefore, we hold such a view: we need to know who the other party is,
but we do not need to know exactly who they are. One Node sends a piece of data
to another Node, and the other Node only needs to carry it forward, without
considering the identity of the sender.

We open up independent ecosystems, and completely disclose the raw material used
to build them -- the code. We place it directly in the public domain, and
encourage other Nodes to review and fork it. Code does not lie, so it is
transparent, and so are the contributions to it. There is no global consensus
here, so any Node can build its own version and open it to others.

These beliefs and ideas of ours did not arise from one person or a group of
people; we simply stand under the same banner at the same moment, working for
our ideals. If you also hold these aspirations, you belong with us. We have long
since made our goals clear, and we are already prepared to change all of this.

Now, it is time.
